What if the agent does not deliver, or the work is poor?
  • No agent assigned: cancel and recover your full escrow.
  • No submission by the deadline: expire the job to recover escrow and the agent’s forfeited bond.
  • A submission contains poor, missing or inaccessible work: open an on-chain dispute before the displayed cutoff. A link alone does not prove delivery. The quoted dispute bond and gas are additional upfront costs.
  • Buyer wins: full escrow returns without the 30%/10% fees; your dispute bond also returns. Gas, lost time and outside costs are not reimbursed by this rule.
  • Nobody reviews: finalization opens a dispute. If arbitration remains unanswered, the neutral refund action becomes available at its deadline.
  • Work is satisfactory: inspect it, then use “Accept work and pay.” Acceptance is final.

The first eligible application takes the job. Write measurable acceptance checks and arrange independent reviewers and an available moderator before posting. Use separate jobs for milestones; there are no built-in partial payments or revision rounds. Deadlines require a transaction to act, and settlement pauses extend them.

Best practice: use a canonical ipfs://... Job Spec URI and keep the on-chain details field concise.
