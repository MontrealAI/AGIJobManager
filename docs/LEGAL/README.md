# Legal and operator protection center

**v1.0.4 · English publication · 18 September 2026**

AGIJobManager is MIT-licensed software. Publishing it does not by itself make MONTREAL.AI the operator, employer, custodian, adviser or guarantor of someone else's deployment. An owner also does not guarantee every job simply by holding an administrative address. Actual activities, control, promises, payments and applicable law determine each person's responsibilities.

**These notices reduce ambiguity; they do not guarantee immunity, regulatory exemption, enforceability or freedom from claims.** The repository supplies no worldwide legal clearance. Independent operators need terms and an operating model appropriate to the places and people they serve.

## A practical, low-administration operating model

1. **Identify the operator once.** Publish its legal identity, contact, chain, verified manager, owner, both fee recipients and terms version. Distinguish the publisher, ENS authority, UI host, reviewers and moderators. Disclose overlapping roles truthfully.
2. **Keep the service scope narrow.** Document the jobs, users and territories served, what the 30%/10% payments fund, and actual support commitments. Code availability is not authorization to offer custody of users' keys, exchange, investments, guaranteed returns or regulated professional work.
3. **Use existing controls.** Keep initial intake paused, NFT admission disabled unless deliberately enabled, settlement available, signers secured and moderation responsibilities assigned. Rehearse refunds and incident response. Do not remove recovery controls to create the appearance of decentralization.
4. **Minimize public data.** Use a business contact; keep confidential work in access-controlled systems. Never put identity documents, secrets or privileged advice on-chain, in ENS/IPFS or in public issues.
5. **Review changes.** Revisit the assessment when territories, users, control, fees, services, data processing or applicable law change. Keep necessary records and one maintained contact route. Do not promise constant support unless it is actually provided.

Complete the [launch checklist](../LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md) and the deployment-specific review before paid intake. Technical readiness is not legal approval. Publication does not adopt an operator's terms or amend earlier contracts, jobs or agreements.
