# Activity and jurisdiction review

**Reference review: 18 September 2026.** This is an operator scoping aid, not a legal opinion or an exhaustive global survey. Recheck authorities before relying on them. Operator location, user location, targeted markets and actual services can all matter; an overseas owner does not settle the analysis.

## Review the system that exists

| Actual feature | Question for the deployment's adviser |
| --- | --- |
| USDC escrow, bonds, refunds and participant payments | Do payment, money-transmission, virtual-asset, custody or other financial-service rules apply, including any relevant exception? |
| 30% / 10% of successful job cost paid to configured wallets | Who ultimately receives the fees, for what service, with what tax/disclosure duties? The fees are not automatically passive software royalties. |
| Owner pauses, eligibility, moderator appointments and stale-dispute resolution | What duties follow from retained control? No proxy and restricted withdrawals do not mean no administrator or intermediary. |
| Hosted console, promotion, ENS admission or paid onboarding | Who offers the service, handles complaints and processes data? A UI restriction cannot block every direct contract call. |
| Employers, agents, reviewers and moderators | What worker, consumer, professional-service and IP rules apply? AI-only intent does not decide legal classification. |
| Public wallet/ENS/URI data and private evidence | Who controls each data flow, purpose, access, retention and lawful basis? Public does not mean anonymous or unregulated. |

Do not claim exemption solely from open-source licensing, automation, decentralization, foreign ownership, optional NFTs or user self-certification. Nor should every publisher or owner be assumed to need the same registration. Record the actual analysis and its conditions.

## Official starting points

| Authority | Relevant distinction; no deployment-specific conclusion is implied |
| --- | --- |
| [US FinCEN, FIN-2019-G001](https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/2019-05/FinCEN%20Guidance%20CVC%20FINAL%20508.pdf), sections 4.4 and 5.2.2–5.2.3 | Mere DApp development differs from using or deploying it for money transmission. Automated owner/operator activity can bring obligations. This guidance is not clearance under all US laws. |
| [Canada FINTRAC, money services businesses](https://fintrac-canafe.canada.ca/msb-esm/msb-eng), payment and virtual-currency sections | The guidance covers virtual-currency transfers and specified payment intermediaries, with stated exceptions. Receiving payment for one's own goods/services differs from facilitating others' payments. |
| [EU Regulation 2023/1114 (MiCA)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng), recital 22 and operative scope | Fully decentralized services without an intermediary are distinguished from activities controlled directly or indirectly by persons. Partial decentralization is not a blanket exclusion; other laws may apply. |
| [Québec Charter of the French language](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/C-11?langCont=en), including section 55 | Assess language requirements for the actual agreement and parties. An English worldwide template does not establish that required French presentation or valid language choice has occurred. |

The earlier [GoalOS legal center](https://montrealai.github.io/GoalOS_Public_Sovereign-Reserve-P-4/governance/LEGAL_INDEX.html) informed the separation of publication, operations and rights. Its research-only service description and restricted reuse license do not describe this MIT-licensed, transaction-capable software and are not imported.

## Proportionate review

Start with the activities and territories actually planned. Give the adviser the code, [owner controls](../OWNER_CONTROLS.md), fee-beneficiary identities, intended users, communications and data flows. Identify and meet applicable authorization, screening, tax, consumer/worker, privacy and language requirements before opening the affected service. Do not collect identity data merely to make an unnecessary compliance claim, or omit verification/records that are required.

Keep the conclusion and assumptions privately; publish a truthful operator notice. Reassess material changes. If requirements cannot be met, keep affected intake closed and seek advice on lawful wind-down while preserving exit rights. ENS, NFT eligibility and a blacklist are not a complete KYC/AML or sanctions program. The readiness script does not check legal compliance.
