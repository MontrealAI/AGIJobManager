# Deployment notice and operator terms template

**Template v1.0.5 — not an adopted agreement or proof of legal clearance.**

Complete this notice for your deployment, remove inapplicable promises and obtain review for the actual business and territories. Publish it before inviting paid use. Do not identify MONTREAL.AI as your operator, guarantor or contact unless that role is actually agreed. Keep keys, identity documents and privileged advice out of public records.

## 2. Service, fees and control

Describe the service actually provided. Identify who agrees scope, deliverables, lawful data access, acceptance criteria, deadlines, IP and confidentiality. Automated agents act for responsible operators; an agent/contractor label does not determine worker classification.

Disclose the full cost before commitment: the per-job validator budget, fixed 30%/10% allocations, agent remainder, separate bonds, gas and applicable taxes. At the default 8% reviewer budget, a successful 100 USDC job allocates 8 / 30 / 10 / 52 before rounding or unallocated reviewer amounts. Explain refunds, neutral timeout, reserved failed payments and recovery limits using the [payout specification](../USDC_PAYOUT_SPLIT.md) and [buyer guide](../BUYER_PROTECTION.md). No notice removes code-supported refunds or mandatory remedies.

Disclose [owner powers](../OWNER_CONTROLS.md), separate helper/ENS powers, immutable settings, actual response coverage and who submits operational transactions. Deadlines do not execute themselves. Do not promise guaranteed quality, returns, insured funds, rescue, uptime or absolute decentralization.

## 3. Candidate risk-allocation clauses for local review

These are proposed clauses for an operator's **validly accepted service agreement**, after local review. They are not extra MIT conditions or already accepted merely by downloading this repository.

- **No implied service promise.** Except for express commitments and duties imposed by law, the operator, owner and moderators do not promise successful work, uninterrupted availability, dispute outcomes, asset recovery or continuing support. Discretion is limited by code and law; this does not waive an accepted duty or excuse misuse of authority.
- **Warranty limits.** Subject to express promises and mandatory rights, supply voluntary services/tools as available without additional warranties of accuracy, fitness, availability or results. The software remains under MIT.
- **Liability limits.** Where enforceable, exclude indirect/consequential loss. If appropriate, insert a locally reviewed aggregate cap with a clear amount, currency, period and scope. Escrow, bonds, beneficiary claims and mandatory refunds are not the operator's funds; a cap does not authorize retaining them. Exclude fraud, intentional misconduct, gross fault, protected injury and all other non-excludable liability as required by law. No clause bars regulators or non-consenting third parties.
- **Professional-user indemnity, if appropriate.** A business user may cover reasonable third-party losses caused by its unlawful content, infringement, intentional misuse or breach, to the extent enforceable. Limit recovery to that user's responsibility; exclude the protected party's own misconduct and non-indemnifiable amounts. Identify beneficiaries, prompt notice, defense control, reasonable cooperation and consent to settlements imposing admissions or obligations. Do not impose unlawful consumer indemnities, non-indemnifiable fines or new MIT conditions.
- **Separate roles; no personal guarantee.** Merely contributing code, holding a role address or signing as an authorized representative does not create a personal guarantee or agency. Actual commitments, authority, conduct and mandatory personal liability remain effective. Only authorized parties can contract for an entity.

Use a lawfully established operating entity, separate business finances and appropriate insurance if advised for the real activities. Incorporation, insurance, multisignature wallets and overseas ownership do not guarantee protection from liability or regulation.

### Privacy-specific user duties and recourse

Incorporate the [user-data rules](USER_DATA_RULES.md) prospectively through a valid agreement: public-content restrictions, user authority and lawful private workflows, agent/provider responsibility, minimal private incident reporting and cooperation. Consider the narrowly scoped business-user recovery clause there, identifying beneficiaries including MONTREAL.AI or the contract owner only where an enforceable arrangement permits. Do not impose automatic escrow deductions or waive data-subject rights. No clause transfers an operator's statutory duties or excuses its own breach.

## 4. Agreement, complaints and language

Choose governing law and dispute procedures only after local review; no single forum is imposed on all deployments. Preserve mandatory consumer/worker forums, lawful complaints, regulatory cooperation and urgent relief. Provide a direct complaints contact and realistic process. No agreement guarantees freedom from investigation or suit.

Present the actual operator, version, fees, powers and terms before seeking meaningful agreement. Use the required language and acceptance process; retain appropriately minimized evidence with a defined retention schedule. A generic transaction, preselected checkbox or unversioned browser flag is not conclusive acceptance. The console checkbox is bypassable and is not an on-chain agreement system.

These materials are English. Where mandatory language rules apply, including applicable Québec requirements, supply required translations and a valid language-choice process. Worldwide availability is not a waiver.

## 5. Privacy and private adoption record

Adapt the [privacy notice](PRIVACY.md) to actual hosts, RPCs, wallets, metadata, support and analytics. Identify responsibility, purposes, lawful basis where required, recipients, retention, rights contact and incident process. Do not promise zero personal data or deletion of public-chain history.

Keep privately with the deployment record: the public notice/version/hash; roles and fee-beneficiary evidence; relevant jurisdictions and review conclusion; required authorizations or an evidenced basis for not requiring them; translations/acceptance process; operating and privacy arrangements; reviewer, date and reassessment triggers. No item is pre-approved by this release. Do not publish privileged advice or identity documents.

Keep intake paused until the operator approves its actual operational and applicable legal requirements. Preserve accrued rights and code-supported exits. Amend terms prospectively through a valid process; a release does not amend existing agreements automatically.
