## Remaining operational and implementation limits

Physical Mac accounts and permissions, provider/model identity, signing-account isolation, multi-hour native work, recovery on real hardware, independent valuable-job outcomes, actual bills, paid settlements and human supervision time remain uncommissioned here. No independent external security review was performed.

The native runtime has whole dedicated-account UI authority. It does not enforce a per-site/business-action credential broker. Broker-scoped/local-only jobs remain unadmitted; copied plaintext cannot be revoked cryptographically. Projects retain one Agent/Node pair and do not automatically authorize repair/reassignment. Native/private on-chain handoff and general automated moderation remain unimplemented.

Capacity estimates trust declared inputs and are not authorization. The schedule assumes one worker per role, reserves full remaining declared work plus a margin, and may conservatively reject a feasible optimized schedule. V2 quality gates do not statistically correct arbitrary correlated errors. Every real production policy requires fresh independently signed evidence for its exact source, runtime and specification; synthetic results cannot satisfy that gate.
