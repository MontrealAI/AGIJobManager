# AGIJobManager v1.19.5 recovery and migration

Keep a verified copy of the original edition, complete campaign, project/history exports, drafts and source-bound qualification evidence before changing checkouts. Inspection needs no wallet, signing key or funded account.

## Reinspect or stop safely

1. Retain the complete original campaign and any draft without reformatting or repairing them in place. Keep their checksums with the original edition’s manifest.
2. Use a verified compatible native Alpha inspector installed by the operator. Obtain expected runtime bindings independently from the current frozen code/model/configuration/evidence context.
3. Reinspect through the [documented API](../../successor/TRANSFORMATIONAL_WORK.md). Rejection, malformed content or stale bindings creates no partial Manager job, reservation, payment or transaction because this adapter performs no persistence or external action.
4. Keep a rejected or stale file as history. Continue only with a new explicitly authorized Alpha campaign revision and comparison basis. Do not rewrite completed approvals, witnesses, budgets, routes, epochs or results.

The native pilot permits zero currency allowances. Do not “recover” a positive paid proposal by raising the cap, copying a digest from the imported file, substituting a policy-double inspector or converting an AGIALPHA amount. Positive native paid proposals remain unsupported. A new route, purpose, permission or budget requires new upstream authorization before execution.

## Preserve existing work

The Builder’s immutable v1.19.4 code pin remains unchanged. Keep its original project/history exports and use its [restore procedure](../../successor/README.md) for supported local projects. This campaign adapter neither imports execution authority nor upgrades the Builder or an old deployed Manager.

Existing jobs, claims, refunds and old review-escrow credits stay with their original contracts and journals. Recover them using the [incident response guide](../../OPERATIONS/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md) and their original source context. A failed deployment command may already have broadcast: inspect its original journal and receipts before retrying. Never replace a source-bound creator campaign or its compiler input with newer application code.

For separately authorized Ubuntu creation, preserve hidden local ephemeral deployer-key entry and the original creator journal. Ledger governance remains on Mac Studio. Never place keys in arguments, dotenv files, shell history or logs. This migration authorizes no deployment, ENS change, token approval, funding or unpause. If qualification or compatibility is uncertain, stop at inert inspection and retain the prior verified edition.
