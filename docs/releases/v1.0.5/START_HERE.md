# AGIJobManager v1.0.5 — start here

This edition reduces accidental public disclosure and documents users' data responsibilities. It preserves MIT licensing and contract behavior. Statutory privacy duties and rights cannot be transferred by a notice, checkbox or indemnity.

1. Verify the downloaded files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The manifest identifies the frozen source, evidence and every archive payload file.
2. Open `agijobmanager-usdc.html`. Read the protocol notices and `source/docs/LEGAL/USER_DATA_RULES.md`. Use only public-safe descriptions, links and receipts. Keep personal information, private work and secrets in a separate authorized workspace.
3. Review each public-content confirmation before posting or completing a job. IPFS upload has its own review of the exact JSON and destination. Cancel sends nothing. Do not treat the check as a content scanner or legal consent receipt.
4. Drafts are saved and loaded only when requested. Completion drafts stay in page memory. Use **Clear saved job content** to remove old saved drafts and notes on this browser origin. Pinning JWTs are never saved by this console; revoke previously exposed credentials through their provider. Browser clearing cannot erase downloaded or public copies.
5. An owner uses `source/docs/LEGAL/OPERATOR_NOTICE_TEMPLATE.md` and `source/docs/OPERATIONS/PRIVACY_RESPONSE.md` to identify the actual operator, applicable roles, providers and private contact. User responsibility/recovery clauses need a valid operator agreement and local review. Do not appoint MONTREAL.AI as an independent service's privacy contact without agreement.
6. For a new deployment, follow `source/hardhat/README.md` and `source/docs/LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`. NFT eligibility starts disabled and intake starts paused. Supply the actual owner and two recipient addresses and complete the deployment's readiness checks.

For an existing compatible manager, use its verified address. Updating the console does not change its state, migrate jobs or amend earlier agreements. Other clients and direct contract calls do not enforce these console safeguards.

The complete ZIP contains the console, source, license, guides, qualification evidence and release tooling. The immutable `v1.0.5` tag pins the application source. Read `RELEASE_NOTES.md` and `VALIDATION.md` for scope. Publication is not an Ethereum transaction, independent audit or worldwide legal clearance.
