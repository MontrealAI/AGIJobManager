## How to use this collection

- Treat an essay’s addresses, token names, payout rules and role descriptions as specific to its cited version.
- Check the relevant repository and release evidence before installation, funding or execution.
- A recorded action, a cryptographic hash or a validator vote supports a bounded claim; reviewer independence, work quality and autonomous execution require their own evidence.
- Economic and civilization-scale scenarios retain their stated assumptions; they are not measured adoption, revenue or production qualification.
